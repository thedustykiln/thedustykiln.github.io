Behind the Apron.

I'm not a potter by profession. In fact, I'm not even an artist by profession... at least, not in the traditional sense; rather, I'm a physicist, dealing in the art of equations and code.

However, I've always loved creating things, exploring ideas, and understanding how simple pieces come together to make something greater. For me, pottery provides the opportunity to slow life down and combine all of these things into one tangible object... well, unless it explodes in the kiln, then it's many smaller tangible objects.

Much of my work is inspire by the outdoors, the quiet simplicity of country living, with occasional playful touches and influences from my life in science. Through The Dusty Kiln, I hope to share what I'm making and inspire others to get outside, experience something new, create something of their own, and pay a little more attention to the world around them.
