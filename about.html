ABOUT US

Made by hand, one piece at a time.

Clay & Co. is a small pottery studio creating functional ceramics inspired by the natural world.

Studio

A slower way of making.

We believe that the objects we use every day should have a sense of character and permanence.

Every piece is thrown, shaped, glazed, and fired by hand in our small studio. Variations in color, texture, and form are part of what makes each piece unique.

Our work is designed to be useful first—and beautiful because of it.
