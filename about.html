ABOUT THE MAKER

A physicist, a handful of clay, and a need to create.

I didn't set out to become a potter. I simply wanted to make something with my hands.

Studio

From numbers to clay.

I'm not a potter by profession. In fact, I'm not even an artist by profession—at least not in the traditional sense. I'm a physicist, and much of my working life is spent in the art of numbers, equations, and code.

But I've always loved to create. Ideas, objects, questions, experiments—anything that starts as something abstract and becomes something real. I've also always been drawn to taking things apart, understanding how they work, and finding the simplest form hidden underneath.

Pottery gives me a place to bring those instincts together. Starting with nothing more than a handful of wet clay, I can turn an idea into something tangible. It's a chance to slow down, work with my hands, embrace a little imperfection, and eventually share the result with someone else.

WHERE IT COMES FROM

Inspired by wide-open spaces and simple things.

I'm originally from Texas, and much of my work draws from the places and experiences that shaped me: the outdoors, the Wild West, and the quiet simplicity of country living.

Every now and then, my other life finds its way into the studio, too. A scientific idea, an interesting structure, or some small detail from the world of physics might find its way into a piece.

I'm excited to share what I'm making and, hopefully, inspire you to get out there, experience something new, make something of your own, and live a little more deliberately.

THANK YOU

Thanks for being here.

Whether you find something for your own home or simply stop by to see what I've been making, I'm glad to share a little piece of the process with you.
